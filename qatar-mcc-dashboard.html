How to read this dashboard

  • Each dot is one staff member who should have an MCC this month. Competent, Needs coaching, Development required (the status given on the MCC form), not yet assessed.
  • Coverage is how many eligible staff have been assessed. Target is 100% every month.
  • Average score is the store's MCC average. Green 85%+, amber 70–84%, red below 70%.
  • Month-on-month chips show the change versus the month you pick in "Compare with". Green means better, red means it dropped.
  • Staff under 3 months' tenure, staff on leave and the store leader are not eligible, so they don't appear.
  • Anyone below 80% is on the Coaching list and needs a GROW plan and a reassessment.
  • Tap a store, then a name, to see the assessor, every remark and the GROW plan. Use the gold buttons to download records as PDF.
